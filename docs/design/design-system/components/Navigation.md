Every page outside the account area opens with the same three lines: `.eyebrow` naming the area, the serif `h1` as a full sentence, then a `.lede`. A `.section-number` sits right-aligned against the heading inside `.page-heading`.

Inside the signed-in account area the heading is different, and deliberately so. The `h1` is the name of the section — exactly the words of the active menu item («Заказы», «MarketMesh ID») — set in the serif at 2.6rem (2.2rem below 720px), with no full stop, no eyebrow and no section number, followed by one `.lede`. The sidebar already says where the reader is; an eyebrow would repeat it and a number would drift out of step with the menu the moment a section is switched off. Use `.account-heading` for it.

Two nav patterns exist, and they are not interchangeable. `.nav-link` is for top-level destinations that exclude each other (the account, sign-in, registration) — active state is a 1px underline in `text-link`. `.account-nav` is for the sections of the signed-in account area. At most one of each per screen.

`.account-nav` is a vertical list in the account sidebar, never a row: a row of sections wraps to a second line as soon as enough of them are switched on, and a wrapped nav reads as two navs. Items are links in a `ul`, each with `11px 14px` padding and an `r-sm` hit area; the active one takes `surface-tint`, `text-link` and weight 600 and carries `aria-current="page"` — background and weight make it a shape, not just a hue, so the state never rests on colour alone. A section may show a count on the right in `text-secondary` and tabular figures (orders, favourites, reviews waiting for a verdict); prefix the number with a visually hidden phrase so it reads «Отзывы, ждут отзыва: 2», and leave the count out rather than showing zero.

The sidebar holds, top to bottom: the section list, the «Тема оформления» select, and a text button «Выйти из аккаунта». Theme and sign-out are controls, not sections — they never become menu items. The select saves itself — half a second after the last change, so arrow keys stepping through a closed list don't send every option, or at once when focus leaves it — and answers with an inline notice under it; the account's other sessions are ended from the sessions section of MarketMesh ID, not from the sidebar.

Across breakpoints: the account sidebar is 268px wide, narrows to 230px between 720 and 1000px, and below 720px sits above the content as the same vertical list, separated by a hairline, with no horizontal scroll at 320px. `.section-number` is hidden below 720px, so nothing may live there that isn't stated elsewhere; the profile grid collapses from `280px minmax(0, 1fr)` to one column at the same width; above 1400px the page gains top padding and the identity column widens to 295px.

Only one `h1` per page. Decorative glyphs (↗ ↳ ✳) are always `aria-hidden`, and the section number is presentational too.

The eyebrow now sets at `eyebrow` (0.73rem) rather than the source's 0.65rem — uppercase at that tracking was the smallest text on the page and the first thing to fail on a dim screen.
